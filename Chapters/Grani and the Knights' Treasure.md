- Memory block/core/key that Kal'tsit needs and absorbs blood.

## Plot
- Grani rescues Carol from bounty hunters searching for a lost treasure on the way to join a conflict.
- They encounter Skadi and Big Bob, the latter of whom they decided to partner with to find the treasure.  
- Grani helps Carol find the treasure to save Dewville from destruction caused by bounty hunters' search. 
- Big Bob betrays them to get treasure to fund a migration to Columbia, while Skadi joins them to get the key to exchange for information on Spectre's ori'm nerve agent. 
- They find the treasure and somehow manage a peaceful resolution between the four of them.
